A LITTLE HELP GOES A LONG WAY

Frequently asked questions.

Ask our team
01

How do I request a quote?

Use “Add to quote” on a featured product to build a list. Open your quote list, copy the product names and include them in your enquiry to the Edtech team.

02

How do I choose the right product?

Start with the product categories or browse by who you’re shopping for. If you need guidance, contact the team with the learning goals and requirements you have in mind.

03

Can I find tools for specific needs?

Browse Specific Needs to explore relevant assistive technology. The team can help you narrow down the options for your individual requirements.

04

How do I check software compatibility?

Check the product page for the supported devices, operating systems and licence options. If anything is unclear, contact Edtech before ordering.

05

Where can I find delivery and returns information?

Visit the Delivery and Returns page linked in the footer for the current information. For a question about a particular order, contact the team.

06

Can I get help getting started?

Contact Edtech with the name of your product and the help you need. Include your device and operating system details for software-related questions.
